v1.1.0
Save compatible. Can be added to existing saves.

- Autoforge can now build fighter wings.
- Colony items installation should be fixed.
- Fixed the refit screen ignoring Expanded Cargo Holds and Additional Berthing.
- Splitting off the Flotilla from the management screen now tells you what your cargo, fuel and crew capacity become once those ships leave.
- You can now manually set how much cargo and quarters each ship converts to the Flotilla (including the flagship). This is actually a substantial buff. I might need to nerf it.
- The Nomad Cargo, Quarters and Full Conversion hullmods are deprecated.
- Round-the-Clock Yards' bonus now ends as soon as you scrap the Work, instead of lingering for a few days.
- Added support for other mods adding their own Works, colony items, and allocations. See MODDING.md.
- Added cross-mod interactions with Captain's Quarters.
- Rewrote the Manual
